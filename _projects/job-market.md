I built this project to understand the data job market better before stepping into it. It uses job-posting data from [Luke Barousse's Python course](https://www.lukebarousse.com/python), which covers job titles, salaries, locations and required skills.

## The questions

1. What are the most in-demand skills for the top three most popular data roles?
2. How are in-demand skills trending for data analysts?
3. How well do jobs and skills pay for data analysts?
4. What are the best skills for data analysts to learn, meaning both high demand and high pay?

## Tools

- **Python**, with **Pandas** for analysis and **Matplotlib** and **Seaborn** for charts
- **Jupyter Notebooks** to keep code, notes and results together
- **Git and GitHub** for version control

The full analysis, code and charts are in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/maytarr/Python_Data_Project).
