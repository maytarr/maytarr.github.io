MedAudit captures medical receipts, reads them on the device, asks a person to review every record, and exports a per-tax-year expense schedule (XLSX) plus one combined evidence PDF.

It organises and reconciles evidence. It does not decide what is tax-deductible or replace professional tax advice.

## Key principles

- **Local-first:** receipt data never leaves the phone. Text recognition runs on the device, with no backend or cloud service.
- **No invented values:** every record is reviewed and approved by a person, and every edit is logged.
- **Traceability:** each row in the expense schedule links back to its receipt in the evidence PDF.
