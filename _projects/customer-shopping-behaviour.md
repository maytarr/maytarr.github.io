This project follows a retail dataset of 3,900 customer purchases from raw data all the way to a business presentation. It's based on [Amlan Mohanty's data analyst portfolio project tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PrZvPeUw60).

## What I did

1. **Cleaned the data in Python:** filled in missing review ratings, added age groups and purchase-frequency fields, and checked for redundant columns.
2. **Loaded it into PostgreSQL** and answered business questions with SQL.
3. **Built a Power BI dashboard** and a slide deck to present the findings.

## The business questions

- Do male or female customers bring in more revenue?
- Which customers used a discount but still spent more than average?
- Which products have the best reviews?
- Does express shipping go with bigger purchases?
- Do subscribers spend more than other customers?
- Which products rely most on discounts?
- How do customers split into new, returning and loyal groups?
- What are the best-selling products in each category?
- Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?

The code, SQL queries, dashboard and presentation are in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/maytarr/customer-shopping-behaviour-analysis).
